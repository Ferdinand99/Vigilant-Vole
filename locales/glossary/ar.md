# Glossary: Arabic (ar)

Address form: Modern Standard Arabic, neutral. Nouns and the masculine singular imperative ("أدخل", "اختر"), or impersonal phrasing. Never formal or stiff.
Quotation marks: « » for names and quoted text. Punctuation: ، ؛ ؟ . Digits: Western digits (0-9) everywhere. No tatweel. Product names, protocols, URLs, file names, code stay Latin.
Plurals: all six CLDR categories. Counts use "{n}" plus the noun in the right form (one/zero/other: singular, two: dual accusative, few 3-10: plural, many 11-99: singular accusative with tanwin, other: singular).
Monitor is treated as a masculine noun (مراقب); its plural is مراقبات.
