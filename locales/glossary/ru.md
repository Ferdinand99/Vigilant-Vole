# Russian (ru) glossary

Style: neutral register, imperative or impersonal ("Введите…", "Не удалось…"); no "ты", "вы" only when unavoidable.
Quotation marks «…» (inner „…“). Plain «е» (no «ё»). Sentence case. Ellipsis `…` kept as in English.
Plurals: one / few / many / other (other = genitive singular, e.g. `{n} монитора`). Kept in English: product, protocol and provider names.
