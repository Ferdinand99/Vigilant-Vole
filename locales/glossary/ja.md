## Style
- Polite but concise: noun-ending labels for buttons and headings; です/ます for sentences. Addressing the reader is avoided (no お客様, no あなた).
- Punctuation: 。、 and 「」 for quotes (English “ ” become 「」); full-width ！？ only where English has them. No spaces between Japanese and Latin/digits.
- Katakana loanwords with long vowel ー (モニター, サーバー, ユーザー).
- Durations: {n}秒 / {n}分 / {n}時間 / {n}日; plural categories: other only.
- Product and protocol names stay in Latin script.
