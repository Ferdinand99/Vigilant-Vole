## Style
- Modern Hebrew, gender-neutral: infinitive (להזין, לשמור) for buttons and actions; impersonal or noun phrases for sentences; masculine singular only when unavoidable.
- Button labels: Save = שמירה, Cancel = ביטול, Delete = מחיקה (noun forms, short).
- Quotation marks “ ” as in English; no niqqud; Western digits; abbreviations with geresh ׳.
- No sentence case issue (no capitals). Mixed-script strings keep Latin terms as written.
- Plurals: one (exactly 1), two (exactly 2), other.
- Dates: `20 באוקטובר 2026`; durations short (שנ׳, דק׳, שע׳, ימים).
