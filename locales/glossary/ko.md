# Korean (ko) glossary

Style: 해요체 for sentences (Apple style), noun-ending labels for buttons, headings and tabs. No exclamation marks.
Quotes: “ ” (and ‘ ’). Particles are never attached to `{placeholders}`; sentences are rephrased (e.g. `“{name}” 저장됨`, `{name}: …`).
Counts: no plural forms; `모니터 {n}개`. Standard loanword spellings (모니터, 서버, 알림). Product and protocol names stay in Latin script.
