# zh-TW glossary (Traditional Chinese, Taiwan)

Style: neutral and concise; labels omit the subject, "你" only where a sentence needs it (e.g. "你的"). Taiwan MOE
punctuation: full-width ，。：；？！, quotation marks 「 」 (nested 『 』), ellipsis …. A space separates digits/Latin from Chinese
(`{n} 個監控項目`, `{n} 秒`), except around full-width punctuation. Taiwan vocabulary (伺服器, 軟體, 網路, 預設,
使用者, 設定, 資料庫), not a conversion of Simplified. Where English uses “ ” around a name, we use 「 」.
Dates: `2026年10月20日`, months `1月…12月`, durations `{n} 秒/分鐘/小時/天`.
